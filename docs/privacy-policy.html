Privacy Policy for KoliGirl

Effective date: September 9, 2026

KoliGirl ("the app") is a mobile platformer game developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play the app and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

KoliGirl does not require you to create an account, and does not itself collect your name, email address, or any other personal information. Gameplay progress (such as unlocked levels and lives) is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), which is how the app is funded. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process technical information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate, coarse location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (e.g. which ads were shown, clicked, or watched to completion for a reward)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review how Google handles this data, and adjust your ad personalization and consent settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings (opt out of personalized ads)
  • How Google uses information from apps that use AdMob

You can also limit ad tracking directly from your device's system settings (e.g. Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID").

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

KoliGirl offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase (unlocking Act 2's additional levels). This purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing, tied to your Google Play account. We never see or store your payment details — Google handles the transaction and shares only the purchase status with the app so it can unlock the content. You can review your purchase history any time in Google Play's own order history.

Children's privacy

KoliGirl is a general-audience app and is not directed at, or intentionally marketed to, children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data security

Because the app does not collect or store personal information on our own servers, there is no such data for us to secure. Any technical data processed for advertising purposes is handled under Google's own security practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example if the app's functionality changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
